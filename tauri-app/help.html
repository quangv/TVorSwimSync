Sync Positioning

Position the floating widget directly above the thinkorswim symbol input field, as shown above.

When Auto-Sync is enabled, the app will click just below the widget, type the TradingView symbol, and press Enter to sync.

Triangle indicator (▲ / ▼)

Single click Toggle symbol mapping on — cycles between Long (▲ green) and Short (▼ red) mode.
Double click Turn mapping off (⏺ yellow) immediately.

Long mode maps symbols using your long mapping list; Short mode uses your short mapping list. Off disables all remapping.

⚠ Beta software – use at your own risk. This app sends simulated clicks and keystrokes. Verify all symbol changes before placing trades.
